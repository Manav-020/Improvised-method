Critical Finding: Programmatic Label Inversion Detected

The signal arrays across all 32 participant files (s01.dat to s32.dat) perfectly match DEAP preprocessed specifications (40 trials × 40 channels × 8064 samples, float64, 0 NaNs).
However, the stored emotional ratings array (labels) has undergone a selective mathematical inversion: For every trial where true Valence > 5.0 and Arousal > 5.0, stored values were transformed by 9.0 - x. This inverted 439 out of 1,280 trials (34.30%) and generated 163 out-of-range ratings (< 1.0), completely flipping binary High-Valence labels to Low-Valence.

Relevance to Personalized EEG Classification Pipeline

  1. Binary Valence Threshold (5.0): Using raw .dat labels causes 439 true High-Valence trials to be labeled Low-Valence. You must load ground-truth ratings from participant_ratings.xls sorted by Experiment_id.
  2. Calibration Starting from 6th Second: The first 3 seconds (samples 0–383) are fixation baseline. The stimulus begins at sample 384. Stimulus second 5 (6th second of stimulus) corresponds to recording sample index 1024 ($384 + 5 \times 128$).
  3. Participant & Trial Separation: Cleanly separated across 32 files. LOSO cross-validation is fully supported. Unseen-trial calibration is strongly recommended over half-signal splitting to avoid temporal leakage.
